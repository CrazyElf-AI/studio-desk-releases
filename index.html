Studio Desk

Studio Desk is the internal app that Amble Artisans, a design and motion studio in Bengaluru, uses to run its work: clients, projects, deliverables and tasks, time tracking, planning, budgets, invoices and approvals. It is used only by the studio's partners and team members, and by clients the studio invites. You can't sign up; a partner adds each person.

Download

For the Amble Artisans team. You sign in with the Google account a partner added for you.

Download for Windows or Mac

On the download page, pick the file ending in win-x64.exe for Windows, mac-arm64.dmg for Macs with Apple chips (M1 and later, 2020 onwards), or mac-x64.dmg for older Intel Macs. Not sure? Apple menu › About This Mac: "Chip: Apple M…" means Apple chip.

Installing on Windows

  1. Open the downloaded .exe. If Windows shows "Windows protected your PC", click More info, then Run anyway. (The app isn't signed with a paid certificate yet; it is safe.)
  2. Studio Desk installs and opens. Click Continue with Google and choose the account a partner added for you.
  3. Studio Desk now lives in the tray, bottom-right near the clock. It starts when you log in to the computer, and updates itself.

Installing on a Mac

  1. Open the downloaded .dmg and drag Studio Desk into Applications.
  2. Open Studio Desk from Applications. The first time, the Mac says it can't check the app. Click Done (or OK), then go to System Settings › Privacy & Security, scroll down, click Open Anyway next to Studio Desk, and confirm with your password. (On older macOS you can instead right-click the app › Open › Open.) You only do this once.
  3. Sign in with Google as above. Studio Desk lives in the menu bar at the top of the screen. If the Mac asks whether Studio Desk may use its "Safe Storage" in the keychain, click Always Allow.
  4. Updates on a Mac: when a new version is out, you get a notice and the menu shows Download update. Download it and drag it into Applications again, choosing Replace. Your sign-in and settings stay.

Using it day to day

  • Timer: press ▶ next to any task, or Ctrl + Alt + T (Mac: ⌘ ⌥ T) from any app to start or stop the timer on what you did last. Right-click the tray icon for more.
  • Closing the window doesn't stop the timer; Studio Desk keeps running in the tray. Ctrl + Alt + S (Mac: ⌘ ⌥ S) opens it again.
  • Quick bar: press Ctrl + Shift + Space (Mac: ⌘ ⇧ Space) from any app and type, for example, “icons done”, “start clinic reel”, “stop” or “log 1h on reel”.
  • Away from your desk? After 10 minutes away with a timer running, Studio Desk asks what to do with the time.
  • Offline: you can still see everything and run the timer; other changes wait until you're back online.
  • Finals: use Upload final. Your file goes to your own Drive folder for review first.
