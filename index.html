What it does

Everything the studio runs on, in one quiet window.

Live for everyone at once. When a partner moves a deadline, your screen changes too.

A timer that never forgets

Start it on any task. It keeps running in the tray, survives restarts, and asks what to do if you've stepped away.

The quick bar

Press Ctrl Shift Space anywhere and type “icons done” or “log 2h on reel”. That's it.

Work, planned

Clients, projects, deliverables and tasks, with the week laid out by person so nobody is quietly overloaded.

Approvals that move

Ask for a change and the partners get it straight away. Clients approve deliverables from their own view.

Finals in Drive

Upload a final and it lands in the right client folder once approved. Everything is backed up to a sheet every night.

Works offline

Lose the Wi‑Fi and you can still see everything and run the timer. Changes catch up when you're back.

Install

Two minutes, once.

After this, Studio Desk starts when you log in.

  1. Download the file ending in win-x64.exe and open it.
  2. If Windows says “Windows protected your PC”, click More info, then Run anyway.
  3. Click Continue with Google and choose the account a partner added for you.
  4. Studio Desk now lives in the tray, bottom-right near the clock. It updates itself.
  1. Open the .dmg (arm64 for Apple chips, x64 for Intel) and drag Studio Desk into Applications.
  2. Open it. The first time, the Mac says it can't check the app: click Done, then System Settings › Privacy & Security › Open Anyway.
  3. Sign in with Google. If asked about “Safe Storage” in the keychain, click Always Allow.
  4. Studio Desk lives in the menu bar at the top of the screen.

Updates on a Mac: when a new version is out you get a notice, and the menu shows Download update. Drag the new version into Applications and choose Replace. Your sign-in and settings stay. Not sure which Mac you have? Apple menu › About This Mac: “Chip: Apple M…” means Apple chip.
