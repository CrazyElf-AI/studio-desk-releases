Privacy policy

Studio Desk · Amble Artisans, Bengaluru · Last updated 22 October 2026

Who this covers

Studio Desk is an internal desktop app run by Amble Artisans for its partners, team members, freelancers and invited clients. It is not offered to the public, and nobody can sign up; a partner adds each person.

What we collect

  • Your Google name and email address, when you sign in with Google. We use the email only to recognise you as someone a partner added, and to show what you're allowed to see.
  • Studio work records that people enter in the app: clients, projects, deliverables, tasks, time entries, leave, invoices, payments and approvals.
  • Files that team members choose to upload as finals.

Google Drive

One studio partner connects the studio's own Google account to Studio Desk. Studio Desk uses that account to put uploaded finals into the studio's Drive folders, to move a file when it is approved or declined, to save a nightly backup of Studio Desk's records as a Google Sheet, and to read a Google Sheet a partner chooses to import. It does not read your personal Drive, and it never changes who a file is shared with.

Studio Desk's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Where it is kept

Records are stored in the studio's Supabase database, which enforces who can read what: team members see only their own work and pay, and clients see only their own projects, files and invoices. Backups are kept in the studio's Google Drive for 30 days. A copy of the records you can see is kept on your own computer so the app works offline.

The assistant

Studio partners can use an assistant inside Studio Desk. When a partner asks it something, the studio's records needed to answer are sent to Anthropic's Claude service to produce the reply. Anthropic does not use this data to train its models.

What we don't do

We don't sell or rent anyone's information, show advertising, or share it with anyone outside the studio except the service providers named above (Google, Supabase and Anthropic), which process it only to run Studio Desk.

Your choices

You can ask a partner to correct or remove your information, or to remove your access, at any time. You can also remove Studio Desk's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
